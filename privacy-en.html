RetroCam Privacy Policy

Effective: October 7, 2026

RetroCam ("the app") is a photo app that recreates old cameras. Your photos stay on your device by default. Only photos you take for a Shared Roll you joined are stored on our server so the members of that roll can see them. There is no sign-up, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number.

1. What we store on our server

DataPurposeRetention
Anonymous user ID (a random ID the app creates)Telling invites, codes and Shared Rolls apart per userUntil you delete it
Invite code, who invited whom, reward history, promo code redemptions, Unlimited Pass voucher periodGranting rewards and vouchers, preventing duplicatesUntil you delete it
Shared Rolls: roll name, the nickname you choose, photos you take for the roll, when they were takenLetting roll members see the photos together (only your own photos before the develop time)Until you leave the roll or delete your data (if the host deletes, the whole roll is deleted)
Reports (reported photo and reason)Reviewing and acting on inappropriate contentUp to 1 year after review

The anonymous ID is not linked to your real identity and is never used for advertising or tracking. Our server runs on Supabase (data center: Seoul, South Korea).

2. Device check (Apple DeviceCheck)

To stop the same device from claiming invite rewards repeatedly, we use Apple DeviceCheck. We only record with Apple whether an invite code has already been used on the device; we store nothing that identifies the device.

3. Data that stays on your device

Photos and videos taken outside Shared Rolls, camera state, your film/instant film/charge balance and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to our server. Deleting the app deletes them. The app downloads new film looks ("Film of the Month") from our server without sending any personal data.

4. Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: taking photos and videos
  • Microphone: recording sound in video mode (kept on device)
  • Add Photos: exporting to the Photos app when you choose
  • Notifications: local reminders such as "your film is developed"
  • Motion: detecting when you shake an instant print (on device only)

5. Payments

In-app purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. The app never receives your payment details.

6. Sharing, ads and analytics

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties. We use no ads, ad tracking or analytics tools. Shared Roll photos are visible only to members invited to that roll.

7. Reporting and blocking

In a Shared Roll you can long-press a photo to report it or hide that person's photos, and you can leave a roll at any time. Reported photos are reviewed and removed when appropriate.

8. Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Delete my data on the server. Your anonymous ID, invite/reward/promo history, vouchers, the Shared Roll photos you uploaded and the rolls you created are deleted from the server immediately. Photos and items on your device stay.

9. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Shared Rolls are private spaces for people who know the invite code.
